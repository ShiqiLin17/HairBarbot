HairBarbot build book

HairBarbot is a Flowbee with a motorized outer guard. The user picks the cutter up and places it on the head, one spot at a time. The app knows which spot it's on and sets the guard to the length the chosen hairstyle wants there, for example a fade from 2 cm to 4 cm. The clipper only runs when the cutter sits flat on the head, in an allowed spot, with the guard already at the right length.

Design change · Nov 26

The cutter no longer rolls over the head on rollers. It gets picked up and placed, spot by spot, and Carlos's outer guard slides on rails to set the length. To know where it is, we combine three sensors: the IMU (which way it points), two force sensors (is it sitting flat on the head?) and ArUco tags on the cutter that the camera sees next to the face. The course isn't ordering parts for us, so we build with what we have first.

01 · Cutter

What you are building

A printed foot clips into the Flowbee's blade window. The outer guard slides on the foot's rails, and the distance from the blade to the guard's scalp face is the hair length left: 1/2" to 1-3/4" (12.7–44.45 mm, a 31.75 mm stroke). In the recommended D2.2 drive, an N20 gear motor with an encoder sits on a bridge across the rail ends; its built-in M4 lead screw pushes a nut in the guard's roof, and a home switch marks 1/2". Two force sensors sit in the scalp face (top-left and bottom-right). Three tag plates (left, right, rear) carry ArUco tags, and a small box holds the XIAO ESP32-S3 and the BNO085 IMU.

From Carlos's CAD (docs/guard-drive-options.pdf). The motor and gear sizes are estimates until the parts arrive. Grease the rails.

02 · Where it is

How it knows where the cutter is

Each sensor answers one question. The force pads say whether the cutter sits flat on the head. The IMU says which way it points, which picks out the spot when it's flat. The tags say where the cutter touches the head in millimeters, whenever the camera can see one next to the face. Details: docs/cutter-tracking.md.

Each loop (50 times a second)

  • Force pads: is the cutter seated?
  • Location from the tags if seen, otherwise the corrected IMU.
  • Look up the guard length the style wants there and send it to the board.
  • Allow the clipper only if every check passes.
  • While cutting on a seated spot, fill in the coverage map.

07 · User experience

What the user experiences

once · 2 min

Scan

Put on a swim cap and do the guided 3-part head scan in the iPhone app. Saved under your name.

step 1 · 30 s

Choose

Pick a style (Fade 2 cm → 4 cm, Even 2 cm, classic, long trim) and adjust five lengths on a 3D head.

step 2 · 1 min

Set up

Face the camera. Place the cutter flat on the top, back, left and right of the head to calibrate.

step 3 · ≈10 min

Cut

Start at the back of the neck. Place the cutter, wait for the guard, squeeze the trigger, lift, move to the next spot. Ends at 97% coverage.
