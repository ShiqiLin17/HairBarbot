ME416 · Lab 3 · Robotic haircutter

HairBarbot build book

A Flowbee vacuum clipper on a robot that orbits the client's head. Every point on the scalp is a yaw angle, a pitch angle and a distance, so the robot's inverse kinematics are just spherical coordinates. A motorized comb sets the hair length at each spot, and the machine stops the clipper the moment it is somewhere it shouldn't be.

01 · Concept

What you are building

The client sits in a chair with their chin on a rest. An arch frame stands over the chair. Under its crossbar a turntable (yaw) carries a curved arc. A carriage rides along the arc from the crown down to the nape (pitch). On the carriage, a radial slide pushes the Flowbee head toward the scalp (rad), and a small lead screw moves the comb sleeve in and out (guard). The arc's center and the yaw axis meet at the middle of the client's head.

All drawings are concept layouts with real target dimensions. Use them to start your CAD, then adjust to the parts you actually buy.

03 · Electronics

Wiring

One Arduino Mega runs the motors and the safety interlocks. A laptop runs the app, the camera and the haircut planner, and talks to the Mega over USB. The E-stop has two contacts: contact A cuts the clipper in hardware, contact B tells the firmware to stop and pull the clipper back.

04 · Code

Software you already have

The code package has the firmware, a bench-test sketch, the PC control software and the client app. It runs today in simulation with no hardware (python app.py --sim), and the end-to-end tests pass: a full fade haircut, a stop when the head moves, a resume, and a stop on a wrong guard length.

How a haircut is planned

  • styles.py: each style is five lengths (top, front, sides, back, nape) plus an optional fade. Lengths blend smoothly between regions.
  • planner.py: 23 strokes, 15° apart in yaw. Each stroke runs upward from the nape or hairline to the crown, against hair growth.
  • kinematics.py: spherical IK, an ellipsoid head model fitted from 4–5 touch points, and the no-cut zone table.
  • head_tracker.py: MediaPipe face mesh + solvePnP. Stops if the head moves 8 mm, turns 6°, or the face is lost.

First things to change

  • Measure D0 on the real machine and put it in config.py.
  • Re-check steps per unit in the firmware if your pulleys or lead screws differ.
  • Add styles that come out of your user interviews.
  • If you change the no-cut zones, change them in both kinematics.py and the firmware.
05 · Safety

Ten ways the clipper turns off

The brief's core rule is that the machine stops the clippers immediately if it is in the wrong spot. The code checks that directly, and several independent layers back it up. Keep all of them.

LayerWhereTrips when
E-stop contact Ahardwareeither E-stop pressed: clipper power is cut directly, even if code is frozen
E-stop contact BfirmwareE-stop pressed or wire broken: everything stops, radial slide retracts

Heartbeatfirmwarelaptop silent for 400 ms while moving or cutting

Mannequin heads only until RASTIC signs off on the safety setup. For the first human tests, use team members, the longest guard, and a person at each E-stop.

06 · Product

What the client experiences

Sebesta wants a product, not a mechanism. This is the flow the app already supports; your interviews should test every step of it.

step 1 · 30 s

Choose

Pick buzz, classic, fade or long trim, then adjust five sliders. A top-down head map shows the length everywhere.

step 2 · 1 min

Sit & calibrate

Chin on the rest. The comb touches the hair at 4–5 points so the machine learns this head's shape.

step 3 · ≈12 min

Cut

Progress bar, live cutter position, pause and resume. The client holds an E-stop the whole time.

step 4

Finish

Arm parks over the crown. Face and ears are trimmed by hand, as with a home clipper kit.

Interview questions that shape the build

  • How do you describe the cut you want: guard numbers, photos, "same as last time"?
  • How often do you cut, and what do you pay? What would make you trust a machine?
  • Is 12 minutes with your chin on a rest acceptable? What would make it comfortable?
  • Which styles matter most? Each answer becomes a preset in styles.py.

Who to interview

People who already cut their own hair or get short clipper cuts every 2–4 weeks: home-clipper users, Flowbee owners, people with buzz cuts and fades. Skip anyone with long styled hair; the guard tops out at 40 mm.
